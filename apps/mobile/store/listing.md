## Full description (4,000 characters)

Grimstat is an unofficial, fan-made army builder and statistics tool for Warhammer 40,000. It
works offline, and everything you make is stored on your phone.

What it does

• Calculator: the damage one unit does to another, as a full distribution rather than an average,
  with the keywords and abilities that change it.
• Armies: build lists, check them against the rules of the edition, and see their points and
  their matchups.
• Collection: the models you own and have painted, filled in a box at a time.
• Codex: every datasheet, weapon, stratagem and enhancement in the data you load, searchable.
• Battle table: a 3D board with true line of sight, cover and measurement.
• Play: a game in progress, with its rounds, scores and log.
• Analyses: how the factions and lists you follow compare.

Your data

Grimstat ships no Games Workshop data. On the Data page you fetch the community sources yourself
(BSData, Wahapedia and the points from the Munitorum Field Manual), and the app builds its own
data from them on your phone. The lists you paste in from tournaments stay on the phone too.

An account is optional. It carries your armies, scenarios, collection and settings to your other
devices. Sign in with an email address. There is no password. The server holds only what you
made, and you can export or delete all of it at any time.

Free, no adverts, no purchases. Grimstat is an independent fan project. It is not endorsed by or
affiliated with Games Workshop. Warhammer 40,000 is a trademark of Games Workshop Limited.
